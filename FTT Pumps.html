FTT Pumps

Engineered for maximum reliability and efficiency. Discover our advanced range of pumps designed for seamless fluid transfer across rigorous industrial applications.

Centrifugal Process Pumps

  • Capacity: Up to 1500 m³/hr
  • Head: Up to 150 m
  • Operating Temp: -20°C to +200°C
  • MOC: Cast Iron, SS304, SS316, Alloy 20
  • Applications: Chemical transfer, cooling water, clear liquids, and general processing.

Rotary Gear Pumps

  • Type: Positive Displacement
  • Max Capacity: Up to 200 m³/hr
  • Discharge Pressure: Up to 14 Bar
  • Max Viscosity: Up to 100,000 cSt
  • Applications: Lubrication systems, thermic fluids, fuel oils, and viscous chemicals.

AODD Pumps

  • Pump Sizes: 1/4" to 3"
  • Max Flow Rate: Up to 1000 LPM
  • Air Pressure Range: 2 to 8 Bar
  • Body Materials: Polypropylene, Aluminum, SS316, PVDF
  • Applications: Slurries, solvents, highly corrosive fluids, and volatile environments.
